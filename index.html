This is not every trial running in India.

ClinicalTrials.gov is the US registry. Many Indian studies are registered only with the CTRI (Clinical Trials Registry – India). If you find nothing here, search ctri.nic.in as well.

Talk to your own doctor first.

Nothing here is medical advice, and no one on this site can enrol you. Eligibility is decided by the trial doctor after examining you. Do not pay anyone simply to secure enrolment. Ask the study team whether there are any travel, tests or other costs before agreeing to take part.
